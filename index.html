Meal 1

🥞
Moong Dal Cheela
Mon · Wed · Fri
~280 kcal
▼
Ingredients
  • 1 cup soaked yellow moong dal
  • 1 green chilli, ginger (small piece)
  • Cumin seeds, salt, turmeric
  • Chopped onion, coriander leaves
  • 1 tsp oil for cooking
How to Make
1
Soak moong dal overnight or 4–5 hours. Drain and grind to a smooth batter with green chilli and ginger.
2
Add salt, turmeric, cumin, chopped onion and coriander to the batter. Mix well.
3
Heat a non-stick pan, spread a ladleful of batter like a thin crepe. Drizzle ½ tsp oil.
4
Cook on medium heat 2–3 min per side until golden. Serve with mint chutney.

Oats Upma / Dalia
Sunday
~250 kcal
▼
Ingredients
  • ½ cup rolled oats or broken wheat (dalia)
  • 1 small onion, carrot, green peas, beans
  • Mustard seeds, curry leaves, green chilli
  • Lemon juice, salt, coriander
  • 1 tsp ghee
How to Make
1
Dry roast oats/dalia for 3–4 min until lightly golden. Keep aside.
2
Heat ghee, add mustard seeds. Once they splutter, add curry leaves and onion.
3
Add vegetables, cook 5 min. Add 1.5 cups water, bring to boil.
4
Add oats/dalia, stir continuously on low flame for 5–7 min until done. Add lemon juice.

Roasted Makhana Recipe
  • 1 cup fox nuts (makhana)
  • 1 tsp ghee, rock salt, chaat masala
  • Black pepper, red chilli (optional)
How to Make
1
Heat ghee in a pan on low flame. Add makhana.

Dal + 2 Rotis + Sabzi + Salad
Mon · Tue · Thu
~450 kcal
▼
Toor Dal (Arhar) Recipe
  • ½ cup toor dal
  • 1 tomato, 1 onion, 2 cloves garlic, ginger
  • Mustard seeds, hing, turmeric, red chilli, coriander powder
  • Fresh coriander, 1 tsp ghee for tadka
  • Salt to taste
How to Make Dal
1
Pressure cook toor dal with turmeric and water for 3–4 whistles until soft.
2
In a pan, heat ghee. Add mustard seeds, hing, garlic, ginger. Fry 1 min.
3
Add onion, sauté until pink. Add tomatoes and all spices, cook until soft.
4
Add cooked dal, adjust consistency. Simmer 5 min. Add lemon juice + coriander.

Brown Rice + Rajma / Chole
Wed · Fri · Sun
~420 kcal
▼
Quick Rajma Recipe
  • ½ cup rajma (soaked overnight)
  • 2 tomatoes, 1 onion, ginger-garlic paste
  • Rajma masala or kitchen king masala
  • Kasuri methi, cream (optional — skip for weight loss)
  • 1 tsp oil, salt
How to Make
1
Pressure cook soaked rajma with salt for 5–6 whistles. Keep aside with water.
2
In a pan, heat oil. Add onion, cook until brown. Add ginger-garlic paste. Cook 2 min.
3
Add tomatoes and all spices. Cook until oil separates from masala.
4
Add cooked rajma with water. Simmer 10–15 min. Add kasuri methi. Serve with brown rice.

Paneer Tikka + Raita
Tue · Thu · Sat
~320 kcal
▼
Paneer Tikka (No-Oven)
  • 150g paneer, cut in cubes
  • Capsicum, onion, tomato chunks
  • Thick curd — 3 tbsp
  • Red chilli, garam masala, cumin, coriander powder
  • Kasuri methi, salt, 1 tsp oil
  • Lemon juice
How to Make
1
Mix curd with all spices. Add paneer and vegetable chunks. Marinate 30 min minimum (overnight = best).
2
Heat a tawa or grill pan on high heat. Brush with oil.
3
Place marinated pieces, cook 3–4 min each side until charred spots appear.
4
Squeeze lemon, serve with mint raita.

Moong Dal Khichdi

Sleep Improvement

From 2–3 AM to 12 AM — a 4-week transition plan

😴 Poor sleep = weight gain. Sleep deprivation spikes cortisol and ghrelin — the hunger hormone. Fixing sleep will directly help weight loss.
🗓️
4-Week Sleep Shift Plan
Gradual — not overnight
1️⃣
Week 1
Sleep by 1:30 AM, Wake by 8:45 AM
Move Instagram time to end by 1 AM. No Netflix after 12:30. Room completely dark.
2️⃣
Week 2
Sleep by 1:00 AM, Wake by 8:30 AM
Begin a 10-minute sleep ritual. Phone plugged in ACROSS the room. Start using lamp instead of ceiling light.
3️⃣
Week 3
Sleep by 12:30 AM, Wake by 9:00 AM
Add haldi doodh before sleep. Body starts recognising the ritual. Avoid afternoon nap more than 30 min.
4️⃣
Week 4
Sleep by 12:00 AM, Wake by 9:00 AM
Maintain this as the new normal. 9 hours of sleep = optimal for her schedule. Don't panic if one night slips.
🌙
Night Ritual (10 min)
11:30 PM every night
1
Wash face + skincare routine (she probably already does this — attach sleep ritual to it).
2
Dim all lights to warm/yellow lighting only by 11 PM.
3
Phone on DND or in another room. Keep a book or journal by the bed instead.
4
Drink haldi doodh. Take 5 deep breaths. Write 1 sentence of gratitude.
5
Room should be cool (24–26°C), dark, and quiet.

Lifestyle Tips

Small habits that create big changes over time

💧 Hydration — The Simplest Fat Burner
Target 2.5–3 litres of water daily. Drink 1 glass before every meal — it reduces calorie intake naturally. Often hunger = thirst. Check hydration before snacking.

🍽️ Mindful Eating Rules
Eat slow. It takes 20 min for fullness signals to reach the brain. Chew every bite properly.

No screens while eating. Distracted eating = 30% more calories consumed on average.

Use smaller plates. Psychological trick that genuinely works. Feels like more food.

Never eat standing up or in a rush — especially dinner.
📵 Screen & Study Balance
Study blocks: 9:50–10:20 AM (fresh mind) + 4:20–4:50 PM (good energy). That's 1 hour daily = 7 hours/week which is solid for self-study alongside clinic work.

Optional: 9:30–10:00 PM for light review. Never study after 10:30 PM — diminishing returns and sleep disruption.
